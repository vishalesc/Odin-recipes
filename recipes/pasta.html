Red pasta

Red sauce pasta is a classic Italian-inspired dish made by tossing your favorite pasta in a vibrant, tangy tomato sauce. It is traditionally flavored with garlic, herbs (basil, oregano), and chili flakes, and can be kept simple or loaded with vegetables and cheese

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Tomatoes
  • Aromatics
  • Herbs and spices
  • cooking fat

Steps

  1. Boil the Pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Cook your favorite pasta (like penne or spaghetti) according to the package instructions until al dente.
  2. Sauté the Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté finely chopped garlic and onions until soft and fragrant.
  3. Add the Tomatoes: in crushed or pureed tomatoes. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs like basil or oregano. Let it simmer on low for 10-15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  4. Combine: Transfer the cooked pasta directly into the simmering sauce. Toss well so every piece is coated, adding a splash of pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
  5. Serve: Plate immediately and top with freshly grated parmesan cheese.
