Chicken biriyani

Chicken biryani is a fragrant, flavorful, and savory dish made with spiced marinated chicken, caramelized onions, and fluffy basmati rice. These layers of ingredients are slow-cooked together in a sealed pot to let the rice absorb all the rich juices and aromatics.

Ingredients

  • Meat & Base:Bone-in chicken pieces, plain yogurt (dahi), and a fresh ginger-garlic paste for the marinade.
  • Rice & Aromatics:Long-grain Aged Basmati rice, sliced onions, and ghee (or cooking oil).
  • Whole Spices:A blend of cinnamon, green cardamom, cloves, bay leaves, and star anise.
  • Powdered Spices: Garam masala, turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder.
  • Fresh Herbs: Fresh mint leaves, cilantro (coriander), and slit green chilies.
  • Garnish:Golden-fried onions (birista) and saffron soaked in warm milk.

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken: Mix bone-in chicken pieces with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chili powder, biryani masala, salt, mint, and lemon juice. Let it rest for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Parboil the rice: Soak and wash basmati rice. Boil water with whole spices (bay leaf, cardamom, cloves) and salt. Cook the rice for 5 to 6 minutes until it is about 70-80% cooked, then drain.
  3. Cook the chicken & layer: In a heavy-bottomed pot, cook the marinated chicken until tender and slightly saucy. Layer the partially cooked rice on top of the chicken. Sprinkle with fried onions, fresh herbs (mint/cilantro), and saffron-infused milk.
  4. Dum (Slow Cook): Seal the pot tightly with a lid and cook on low heat for 15-20 minutes to meld all the flavors and complete the cooking process.
