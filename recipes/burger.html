Cheese burger

A cheeseburger is a type of hamburger featuring a ground meat patty (typically beef) topped with melted cheese, served inside a sliced bread roll or bun.

Ingredients

  • Meat: Ground beef (80/20 lean-to-fat ratio is best for juicy burgers).
  • Cheese: Classic melting slices like Cheddar, American, Swiss, or Provolone.
  • Buns: Soft burger buns (like brioche or sesame seed), lightly toasted.
  • Seasoning: Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper.
  • Condiments: Ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise.
  • Veggies: Crisp lettuce (like iceberg or romaine), sliced tomatoes, and red onion.

Steps

  1. Prep the Meat: Form \(150 \text{ g}\) to \(200 \text{ g}\) of ground beef into a loose ball. Press it gently into a patty, making the center slightly thinner than the edges to prevent it from puffing up while cooking.
  2. Season: Season generously with kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper right before it hits the pan.
  3. Sear: Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Add a drop of oil, place the patty in the pan, and sear for 3–4 minutes without moving it to get a deep, crispy crust.
  4. Flip & Melt: Flip the burger, immediately add a slice of your favorite cheese (like Cheddar, American, or Swiss), and cover the pan with a lid. Cook for another 2–3 minutes until the cheese is perfectly melted.
  5. Toast the Buns: Lightly butter the insides of your burger buns and toast them in a dry skillet for 1–2 minutes until golden.
  6. Assemble: Layer your bottom bun with your favorite condiments (like a mix of ketchup and mayo), add lettuce, the cheesy patty, tomato slices, and top it off.
